        Uncertainty

        U: none. This estimator holds no uncertainty. It is a fixed-gain blend with no covariance (), so there is nothing to draw as an envelope. The sensor noise settings in the case file are the simulator's noise, not the controller's belief about its own error, and are not used here as bounds.

        Limitations

        • Idealised model: contact is the nozzle plane reaching the ground, with no contact dynamics, and nothing is validated against flight data.
        • A single stored run. It shows one trajectory, not how often this happens.
        • Recorded by the earlier host. The provenance section says what that does and does not establish.
        • Estimates and observations are recorded every , one control cycle in ; the controller runs every and the host checks every step. Markers are recorded values and the thin connector is a reading aid, not data: between two samples nothing is recorded. Events between samples are drawn at their recorded times.
        • The touchdown sample is off the sampling grid. Its truth is interpolated by the host. Its estimate and observation are those of the last control cycle before contact, not of the touchdown instant.
        • No covariance exists, so no uncertainty is shown. No counterfactual run exists, so nothing here shows what would have happened without the fault.
        • Quantities tagged D-view are computed by this view's extractor from recorded values; where tagged approximate they use a proxy for terms the run does not record.
